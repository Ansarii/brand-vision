    Technical & Production Standards | 4 Min Read

    OOH Structural Specifications: GSM Grammage, Wind Loads & Monsoon Preparedness

    Outdoor media displays across South India and the Deccan plateau experience high-heat summers exceeding 42°C and high-velocity wind gusts during the Southwest monsoon season. To guarantee color permanence, taut structural geometry, and strict public safety across our 120-foot spans, Brand Vision enforces the following physical benchmarks:

    • Substrate Grammage & Tensile Strength: We mandate heavy-duty frontlit flex vinyl with a minimum rating of 340 to 450 GSM. Low-gauge vinyl stretches, sags, and tears under transverse wind pressure. Heavy-gauge substrate ensures dimensional stability without distortion across multi-lane sightlines.
    • Inks & UV Stabilization: Solvent and UV-curable industrial inks are calibrated specifically for exterior UV indices. Standard commercial inks degrade by up to 35% in chroma within 30 days of sun exposure; our UV-stabilized formulations maintain color consistency throughout extended campaign flights.
    • Fabrication & Wind Flaps: Large-format unipoles (elevated at 40 to 60 feet) utilize heavy-duty perimeter eyelet spacing every 12 inches and engineered wind-relief cuts in non-critical background sections to relieve aerodynamic drag while retaining graphic legibility.
    • Illumination Engineering: Static hoardings are fitted with calibrated commercial LED floodlights linked to astronomical timers, delivering a uniform 150 to 200 lux surface illumination with zero dark corners or glare streaks.
    Media Strategy & Audience Analytics | 5 Min Read

    Airport DOOH vs. Highway Arterials: Audience Mindset & Dwell Time Analysis

    Brand planners frequently weigh the distinct merits of high-dwell airport media against high-frequency arterial highway hoardings. Rather than viewing them in isolation, high-converting outdoor strategies combine both formats to capture the consumer mindset at different points in their daily journey:

    • Airport DOOH (Concourse LED, Baggage Carousels & Gate Totems):
      Delivers captive, low-distraction exposure to senior corporate executives, tech founders, and affluent travelers. Average dwell times exceed 28 minutes at baggage belts and 35 to 45 minutes at boarding gate lounges, providing deep brand immersion and high messaging retention.
    • Highway Arterials (The Trilight Kokapet & Elevated Flyovers):
      Delivers high-velocity reach across primary commercial lifelines (140,000+ daily vehicles along the Outer Ring Road and Financial District). Massive 4,200 sq.ft physical canvases and 500-meter straight sightlines build undeniable market stature and rapid brand recognition across daily commuters.

    Planning recommendation: Deploy Airport DOOH for detailed narrative positioning and luxury prestige, while securing arterial highway sites to dominate the physical cityscape and drive mass top-of-mind recall.

    Operations & Governance | 3 Min Read

    Proof of Performance (PoP): Standardizing Audit Timelines in Outdoor Media

    Accountability in outdoor advertising requires transparent, time-stamped proof of mounting. At Brand Vision, every scheduled display follows an audited operational flight protocol:

    • Mounting Window: Once production proofs are approved, flex curing and night mounting occur within 72 hours using certified crane rigs and trained rigging personnel.
    • Digital Audit Dossier: Within 24 hours of going live, clients receive comprehensive daytime and fully illuminated nighttime inspection photographs.
    • EXIF Verification: Inspection photos include embedded date, timestamp, and GPS coordinates confirming exact location parameters and illumination status.
    • Weekly Health Checks: Field operations teams perform ongoing bi-weekly site audits to monitor structural tautness, cleanliness, and luminaire performance throughout the contract period.

    Standard commercial, production, flight scheduling, and statutory terms governing media campaigns managed by Brand Vision Media.

    Inventory Allocation & Booking Confirmation

    Brand Vision reserves and manages outdoor hoarding spaces, unipoles, and digital airport displays specified in the official Campaign Flight Schedule. Media inventory is secured solely upon receipt of a countersigned estimate, valid corporate Purchase Order (PO), or advance booking confirmation. Unconfirmed inquiries or informal holds are released after 48 business hours.

    Creative Artwork Delivery & Lead Times

    Clients are required to furnish high-resolution artwork adhering strictly to site technical dimensions (minimum 300 DPI at 1:10 scale in CMYK color space for flex printing; 4K MP4 format for digital screens). Final production files must be delivered to Brand Vision at least 4 business days prior to scheduled flight start date to guarantee on-schedule printing, finishing, and structural mounting.

    Proof of Performance & Flight Commencement

    The contractual campaign period commences on the day the physical display is mounted and illuminated, or digital broadcast commences on the network. Brand Vision delivers verified digital Proof of Performance (PoP) documentation, comprising daytime and illuminated nighttime photographs with date, time, and location records, within 24 hours of installation.

    Illumination & Maintenance Commitments

    Illuminated displays operate during standard commercial evening hours (dusk to 11:30 PM daily or through dawn in accordance with municipal directives). In the event of temporary localized electrical grid failure or adverse weather damage, maintenance crews are dispatched within 24 hours. For cumulative dark hours exceeding 48 hours within a billing cycle, pro-rata campaign flight extensions are credited at no extra charge.

    Cancellation, Modifications & Billing Terms

    Due to strict flight calendar reservation commitments, cancellations requested less than 14 calendar days prior to flight start are subject to a 50% reservation retention fee. Cancellations submitted after flex printing has commenced are billed for 100% of production disbursements. Invoices are payable according to agreed payment milestones; statutory taxes (GST) are applied at prevailing rates.

    Brand Vision collects strictly business-to-business (B2B) information submitted voluntarily by corporate representatives, media buyers, and brand managers. This includes:

    • Contact Details: Representative name, corporate email address, business telephone number, and job title.
    • Company Profile: Legal corporate entity name, industry sector, and office address.

    • Generating billboard mockup visualizations and location maps for client review.

    • Managing day-to-day campaign inquiries initiated by your organization.

Data is accessible solely to authorized Brand Vision media planners and account coordinators.

    Direct Communication & Opt-Out Rights

    We communicate solely regarding ongoing commercial proposals, active bookings, and scheduled deliverables. You may opt out of future planning correspondence or request complete deletion of your corporate contact records at any time by contacting info@brandvisionhyd.com.

    Methodology, regulatory citations, and data sources for all traffic volumes, terminal audience figures, and technical dimensions displayed across our website.

    Airport Passenger Volumes (2.8M+ Monthly Travelers)

    Passenger traffic counts, seasonal flyer densities, and terminal movement figures for Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA Shamshabad) are compiled from publicly documented aviation traffic reports published by:

    • Airports Authority of India (AAI) - Monthly Civil Aviation Performance Summaries.
    • GMR Hyderabad International Airport Ltd (GHIAL) - Terminal passenger statistics and operational press disclosures.
    • Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) - City-pair passenger traffic analysis.

    Arterial Road Vehicular Counts (110,000 - 140,000+ Vehicles/Day)

    Vehicular volumes across key Cyberabad and Hyderabad growth corridors (including Outer Ring Road, Financial District Arterial Stretch, Kokapet Golden Mile, and Narsingi Interchange) are sourced from:

    • Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA) - Comprehensive Transportation Study (HCTS) vehicular census.
    • Hyderabad Growth Corridor Limited (HGCL) - Outer Ring Road automated toll plaza volume audit records.
    • Automated loop detector and CCTV vehicle-classification samples conducted during peak transit windows (08:30-11:30 and 17:30-20:30).

    Airport Terminal Audience Dwell Times (28 - 45 Minutes)

    Audience dwell benchmarks are derived from standardized aviation operations data for passenger journeys through RGIA Terminal 1:

    • Arrivals Baggage Claim: 28-minute average wait duration from aircraft landing through domestic baggage carousel completion.
    • Departure Boarding Gates 101-112: 35 to 45 minutes average seated dwell in hold rooms before flight boarding begins.

    Billboard Physical Dimensions & Sightlines

    All hoarding surface areas, unipole elevations, and screen aspect ratios are physically surveyed and recorded under structural safety engineering approvals:

    • The Trilight Kokapet Mega Hoarding: 120 ft width × 35 ft height (4,200 sq.ft display face; straight-line unobstructed sightline >500 meters).
    • Airport Approach Sequential Unipoles: 60 ft width × 30 ft height per face (1,800 sq.ft across 4 consecutive frames).
    • RGIA T1 Departure Concourse: Custom 4K curved LED display spanning the central passenger artery to Gates 1-22.

    Illumination & Photometric Specifications

    Site lighting specifications are measured using calibrated digital lux meters. Nighttime frontlit hoardings maintain 150 to 200 lux uniform surface illumination via commercial LED floodlights; indoor digital terminal displays operate at 1200+ nits calibrated to terminal ambient lighting.
